SharePoint branding and migration to modern SharePoint

Microsoft ended support for SharePoint Server 2016 and 2019 on July 14, 2026. Your sites still run, but there are no more security updates or fixes. And custom master pages and page layouts don’t carry over to modern SharePoint, so branded sites usually need rebuilding.

What I look at

Every site gets one of three verdicts

Carries over. Needs rebuilding. Retire.

  • Your SharePoint version and the customisations in use
  • Branding: master pages, layouts and styling
  • Workflows and forms, which I flag but do not rebuild
  • Content volume and digital assets, because they drive migration time

Beyond SharePoint

AEM and Sitecore

AEM

AEM 6.5 on-premises core support ends on February 28, 2027, with extended support (critical fixes only) to February 28, 2028. I do AEM branding, custom layouts, content authoring, page building, and components with custom features.

Sitecore

Sitecore 10.0 and 10.1 leave extended support on December 31, 2026. I do content migration from SharePoint, branding, components, authoring and publishing.
