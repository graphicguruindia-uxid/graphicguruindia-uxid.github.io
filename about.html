About

One person, twenty years of CMS work.

I’m Vinod, a CMS specialist in Hyderabad. For 20+ years I’ve made websites and intranets look like the companies that own them, first on SharePoint, then on AEM, Sitecore, WordPress and OpenCMS.

I’ve branded about 120 SharePoint sites and moved about 100 Google Sites into SharePoint Online. I’ve also delivered about 65 2D and 3D projects, including educational animations for CBSE and state syllabus content, and 3D models for Google Earth with a KML animation of the Mars Rover.

I work alone, on purpose. You talk to the person who does the work. I take on a few jobs at a time, and I tell you when I’m full.
